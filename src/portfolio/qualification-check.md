## Case Study: Qualification Check

### Client Overview

**Client:** Qualification Check  
**Industry:** Credential Verification Services  
**Project Brief:** Modernise WordPress site design, develop new content management features, and expand platform to serve both business and consumer markets

### Approach

#### Discovery & Planning Phase

- **Platform Assessment:** Conducted comprehensive audit of existing WordPress infrastructure to identify modernisation opportunities
- **Stakeholder Collaboration:** Worked closely with internal teams and external marketing agencies to align technical improvements with business goals
- **Market Expansion Strategy:** Analysed requirements for targeting growing consumer market alongside existing business clients

#### Design & Development Phase

{% blogImage image2, imageAlt2 %}

- **Modern Interface Design:** Implemented contemporary design patterns that improve user experience for both business and consumer audiences
- **Custom Feature Development:** Built bespoke WordPress features enabling the team to easily manage complex verification services and content
- **Information Architecture:** Restructured site navigation and content organisation to help business clients find information more efficiently

#### SEO & Marketing Integration

- **Technical SEO:** Implemented comprehensive SEO improvements including site speed optimisation, schema markup, and technical best practices
- **Cross-Agency Collaboration:** Coordinated with their marketing team and external agencies to ensure seamless integration of SEO strategies
- **Performance Monitoring:** Established tracking and analytics systems to measure improvements in search visibility and user engagement

### Ongoing Partnership

This represents an ongoing relationship where we continuously evolve the platform to meet changing business needs. Regular updates include new feature development, performance optimisation, and strategic enhancements that support both their established business client base and expanding consumer market presence.
